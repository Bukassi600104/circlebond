Milestone 13 — Circle Completion, Archive, and Data Retention

Objective

Implement the final lifecycle stages and privacy rules.

Completion Types

Gift Circle:

Gift purchased.

Gift delivered.

Aso-Ebi Circle:

Fabric distributed.

Fabric collected.

Deliveries completed.

Support Circle:

Support delivered.

Campaign completed.

Closing Behaviour

When completed:

Contributions lock.

New receipts stop.

Membership changes stop.

Announcements and comments become read-only.

Circle summary remains visible.

Thirty-day retention countdown begins.

Retention Policy

Thirty days after completion or cancellation, delete:

Receipt files.

Receipt notes.

Circle comments.

Temporary communication content.

Temporary gift, Aso-Ebi, or support uploads where the retention policy requires deletion.

Retain only approved minimal records:

Circle ID.

Circle type.

Creator ID where legally and operationally appropriate.

Completion date.

Member count.

Pricing plan.

Aggregated non-sensitive analytics.

Required audit records.

Important Refinement

Profile images belong to the user account and are not deleted merely because one circle closes.

Brand and product images may be retained while another active circle references them. File deletion must be reference-aware.

Required Background Job

A scheduled retention job must:

Find eligible circles.

Delete private files.

Remove or anonymise records.

Record deletion results.

Retry failed deletions.

Prevent orphaned storage files.

Acceptance Criteria

Completed circles are read-only.

Retention countdown is displayed.

Data is purged automatically.

File deletion is verified.

Shared files are not deleted prematurely.

Purge failures generate alerts.

Retention tests pass.
