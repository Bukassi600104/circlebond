MODEL-SPECIFIC PRICING LAUNCH ADDENDUM

Launch scope includes mode-specific pricing cards, one-time trial eligibility,
free draft saving, activation-required publication, safe payment pending and
failure states, same-mode upgrades without data loss, historical activation
pricing, server-side feature gates, a grandfathered legacy path, and a private
owner pricing/activation ledger. A paid activation provider is a hard launch
gate; production must fail closed rather than simulate success when absent.
