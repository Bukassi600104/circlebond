# Pricing and trial help

## Who pays BondCircle?

Only the circle creator pays the one-time activation price for a paid circle.
Members, contributors and invitees do not pay a BondCircle platform fee.

## Is this a subscription?

No. One activation applies to one circle. There is no recurring plan charge in
the approved launch model.

## Can I prepare a circle before paying?

Yes. Creating and saving a draft is free. A paid circle is published only
after its activation payment is verified.

## How does the free trial work?

Each creator account may publish one basic trial circle across Gift, Aso-Ebi or
Support. It allows three total members. Deleting or cancelling that circle does
not restore the trial.

## What happens if checkout is unavailable?

Your draft remains safe and unpublished, and BondCircle does not report a
charge or successful activation. Try again only after the app confirms that
secure checkout is available.

## Can I upgrade?

An active circle can move to a higher tier within the same circle type. The
checkout shows the difference after crediting the historical list price of the
current verified activation. Existing circle data is preserved. Active
downgrades are not supported.

## Does BondCircle take money from contributions?

No. BondCircle does not hold, pool, escrow or take a percentage from circle
contributions. Contribution receipts are coordination evidence and cannot be
used as proof of a BondCircle activation payment.

## What happens to circles created before this pricing model?

They remain grandfathered with their stored capacity and previous
functionality. Completed and archived circles stay read-only.
