# BondCircle pricing note for investors

BondCircle uses a transparent, one-time creator fee per published circle—not a
subscription and not a percentage of community contributions. Contributors
pay the organiser directly and are never charged a platform fee.

Gift Circle is priced at ₦1,500 / ₦3,500 / ₦7,500; Aso-Ebi Circle at ₦3,500 /
₦7,500 / ₦15,000; and Support Circle at ₦1,000 / ₦2,500 / ₦5,000 for Starter,
Standard and Premium respectively. Capacities are 10, 30 and 100 total members.
A creator receives one basic three-member first-circle trial across all modes.

Revenue reporting recognizes only verified activation and upgrade payments.
Contribution amounts and receipts are operational circle data and are never
counted as BondCircle revenue. Historical activation prices are immutable for
auditability and cohort analysis.
