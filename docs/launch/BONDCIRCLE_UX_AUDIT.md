## Privacy workflow contract

- `GET /api/account/export` authenticates the caller and binds every query to that UID, irrespective of supplied URL identifiers. Returns a private, no-store JSON attachment containing profile/preferences, own memberships/contribution metadata, own receipts metadata, authored content, notifications, own activations and auth events. It excludes credentials, push/invitation tokens, storage paths, receipt image binaries and other participants' personal records.
- Pagination continues until all collections finish. Exports above 4 MB or 10,000 records fail explicitly and offer assisted processing; they never return a silent partial success. The export is read over paginated queries, not advertised as a transactionally frozen database snapshot.
- `GET/POST /api/account/privacy-requests` provides private status and authenticated, CSRF-protected intake for assisted export or deletion. Concurrent repeated intake resolves to the persisted request. Database failure does not claim success.
- A deletion request **does not erase an account**, remove memberships, revoke tokens, cancel circles, consume/reset a trial or change historical activation/contribution records. The UI says the account remains active pending review.
- `/owner/privacy` requires existing owner access, records purpose-limited queue access and can acknowledge processing. Acknowledgment and its immutable owner audit insert share a transaction; missing references fail. No UI/API operation marks a request fulfilled or claims that erasure happened.
- Identity-verification procedures, fulfillment operator, response timetable, image-copy delivery and lawful retention/erasure exceptions remain **BLOCKED** pending owner/professional policy approval. Processing and completion of real requests need approved operational procedures. No new policy, contact, SLA or erasure semantics was invented.
- The additive table/connector must pass final migration review, backup and approved staging/deployment gates before these operations can be accepted in production. No live migration or customer operation was executed.

## Current comparative research

Reviewed current primary product/help pages. [Partiful's RSVP flow](https://partiful.com/invitations/online-rsvp) emphasizes link-based browser participation. The applicable inference is to preserve invitation destinations through authentication and policy review, while keeping BondCircle's required verified identities.

[Splitwise's getting-started guide](https://kb.splitwise.com/getting-started/how-do-i-use-splitwise) distinguishes expense/payment recording and external settlement. BondCircle must continue to identify organizer-confirmed records without implying independent bank settlement.

[Givebutter's receipt guidance](https://help.givebutter.com/en/articles/2219216-how-to-manage-automatic-receipts-and-thank-you-messages) connects transaction context, notices and account preferences. The applicable inference is clear statuses and existing preference/navigation access; it does not justify copying its payment model, visual design or features.
