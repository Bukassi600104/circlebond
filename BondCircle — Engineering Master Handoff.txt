Executive Summary

BondCircle is a production-ready, mobile-first Progressive Web App (PWA) for organizing collaborative Gift Circles, Aso-Ebi Circles, and Support Circles.

The application does not custody funds or operate as a wallet in Version 1.

Its responsibility is to organize people, track contributions, coordinate communication, and provide transparency throughout the lifecycle of a circle.

This is a production build, not a prototype, proof of concept, or UI demo.

Help people coordinate celebrations and support initiatives through a trusted, elegant, and simple collaboration platform.

MODEL-SPECIFIC PRICING ENGINEERING ADDENDUM

`lib/circle-pricing.ts` is the versioned catalogue. All prices are NGN minor
units. `server/pricing/entitlements.ts` is the authorization boundary for
capacity and features. `CircleActivation`, `CreatorTrialUsage`,
`PricingPlanDefinition` and `PricingChangeAudit` preserve trial, payment,
upgrade and historical-price facts. New paid circles stay draft until a signed
provider callback completes the activation transaction. Never use a
contribution receipt as activation evidence. Existing records remain on the
legacy/grandfathered path. Operational details and migration rules are in
docs/MODEL_SPECIFIC_PRICING.md.
