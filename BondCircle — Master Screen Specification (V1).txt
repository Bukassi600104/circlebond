MODEL-SPECIFIC PRICING SCREEN ADDENDUM

Each circle creation flow must show cards specific to its selected mode. A
card contains tier, one-time price, total-member capacity, co-admin count,
mode-specific limits, principal inclusions, meaningful exclusions and upgrade
availability. The sequence is type, details, plan, review, save draft,
configure/invite, activate and publish. Draft and payment states are explicit.
An unavailable provider shows a compact error and never a success state. Active
creators may open same-mode higher-plan options showing the exact difference.
Owner administration includes a private Pricing section for catalogue,
trial, activation, upgrade, historical-price and verified revenue data.
